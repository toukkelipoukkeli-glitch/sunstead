# Mission 01: Landscape

## Target

Find the strongest idea for Tangled by mapping nearby companies, startups, OSS projects, research concepts, and internet discourse around agentic code collaboration, autonomous PRs, code review, developer trust, provenance, decentralized forges, and protocol-native open-source state.

The mission should identify what is crowded, what is already solved, what is only adjacent, and where Tangled/AT Protocol creates a genuinely different product wedge. The output should directly inform a 4-minute hackathon pitch and a polished seeded demo.

## Success Criteria

GOLD: landscape is broad, sourced, and yields a specific Tangled-native idea with clear competitor contrast and demo flow.

SILVER: landscape is broad enough to avoid obvious duplicates and sharpen current Patch Passport / Review Passport direction.

BRONZE: territory is mapped but best idea remains uncertain; next mission bottleneck is clear.

## Kill Conditions

1. If the strongest idea is indistinguishable from GitHub/GitLab AI review tooling, kill or reframe it.
2. If the pitch cannot show Tangled/ATProto primitives in the first minute, downgrade and pivot.
3. If a commercial player already owns the exact idea and Tangled adds no defensible wedge, move to a more protocol-native variant.
