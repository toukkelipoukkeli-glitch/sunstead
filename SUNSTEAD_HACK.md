# Sunstead Hack — Event Info

Key logistics, rules, and challenges for Sunstead Hack (Hack Junction), transcribed from the printed program.

## Travel Information

### Helsinki → Levi (outbound)
- The train to Lapland leaves **Helsinki Central Station at 23:13** — be there **before 11 PM**.
- We travel in **2-bed cabins**. Some cabins have a private toilet and shower, some have a shared bathroom.
- The train arrives in **Rovaniemi at 11:00**, and we continue **by bus** to the hotel.

### Levi → Helsinki (return)
- We leave the hotel on **Friday June 26th at 6:30 AM by bus**. Hotel breakfast is served from **5:45 AM**.
- Food is available to buy on the train, but eat well beforehand and/or pack snacks.
- The train departs **Rovaniemi at 9:22** — find your assigned seat on your ticket.
- We return to **Helsinki Central Station at 17:40 on Fri June 26**.

## Pitching
- The **top 3 of each main challenge** and the **top 2 of each side challenge** will be pitching.
- Finalists are selected by the **partners themselves**.
- You have **4 minutes to pitch** and **1 minute for questions**.

## Challenges

### Main challenges
- **Aiven** — Aiven MCP is the bridge. The problem is yours to pick; make the agents work together to solve it.
- **Tangled** — Build an integration on the AT Protocol that plugs into Tangled and improves the open-source workflow. Come with an idea and let the protocol do the heavy lifting.

### Side challenges
- **ElevenLabs** — Every participant gets a free month of Creator tier. Give your project a voice. Everyone walks away with something; the best walk away with a lot more.
- **Anthropic** — Two days and Claude. Three ways to win.
